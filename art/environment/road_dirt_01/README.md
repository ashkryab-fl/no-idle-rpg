# Dirt road

`assets/environment/road_dirt_01.png` is a 2172 × 724 RGBA PNG generated by
the built-in image_gen tool. Original alpha is preserved. The generated road
occupies the middle of the canvas with transparent margins.

DesktopGame reads the central source region (0, 217, 2172, 290), avoiding the
large empty margins without modifying the PNG. ScrollingEnvironment draws it
at y=166 with a 400 × 24 footprint, behind characters and above scenery.
Only about four logical pixels overlap the landscape; actors stand at y=179.
The offset uses the same travel distance as the background, so it stops during
combat and rest. Background and road have independent saved opacity values.
