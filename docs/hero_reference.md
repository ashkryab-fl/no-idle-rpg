# Heroine: References and Generation Guidelines

**Use the images in [`art/hero/reference/`](../art/hero/reference/) for every new generation of
heroine images, poses, animation frames, and sprite sheets.** These are the
primary visual references for her appearance, proportions, hairstyle, costume,
armor, and palette. The written description below supplements the images; if
it does not capture a detail accurately, the relevant image takes priority.

## Primary Images

| File | What it shows | What to use it for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [`reference/front.png`](../art/hero/reference/front.png) | Full-body front view with arms extended in a T-pose | Face, eyes, bangs, breastplate, emblems, belt, front clothing layers, and overall proportions |
| [`reference/back.png`](../art/hero/reference/back.png) | Full-body rear view with arms extended | Ponytail fastening, hair volume and length, back, cape folds and tattered hem, and rear armor details |
| [`reference/side.png`](../art/hero/reference/side.png) | Two side views, facing left and right | Facial profile, breastplate projection, armor layers, hairstyle depth, spacing between hair and cape, and boot and heel profiles |

Use all three files together when the tool supports multiple references. For a
side-view frame, specify which of the two views in `side.png` is needed; both
figures in that file depict the same heroine. If only one reference is supported,
choose the view closest to the target angle and include the description of her
unchanging features from this README.

These references are illustrations rather than precise orthographic drawings:
minor overlaps, strand asymmetry, and accessory placement may vary between views.
Use `front.png` as the primary guide for the face, front emblems, and belt
accessory placement; use the relevant side or rear view for details hidden from
the front. Do not invent new elements to fill areas obscured by hair or the cape.

## Detailed Appearance Description

### Overall Design and Proportions

- A stylized fantasy warrior heroine in an anime/chibi style: a large head,
  compact torso, small hands, and limbs that are short relative to her head.
  These are illustration proportions; the references do not specify her age.
- A compact silhouette with a narrow waist, rounded facial contours, and
  expressive large eyes. Armor adds volume to her shoulders, forearms, and
  shins without making her look like a bulky knight.
- The main recognizable shapes are a very large pale ponytail behind her head
  and back, a red cape, a silver breastplate, a short white skirt with red
  panels, dark stockings, and silver armored boots with gold trim.
- In the reference pose, she stands upright with her feet slightly apart and
  her arms extended sideways. The T-pose makes the character's construction
  readable; it is not a requirement for future illustrations or animations.

### Face and Skin

- Fair skin with a warm peach tone and soft pink-peach shadows. Her cheeks
  have a subtle pink blush and small light accents.
- The face is broad in its upper portion, with soft cheeks, a short lower
  portion, and a small chin. In profile, the nose and chin project subtly;
  she has neither a long nose nor a sharply defined jaw.
- Very large eyes with dark brown upper iris areas and amber-gold lower areas.
  Dark pupils, prominent white catchlights, a strong dark upper eyelid outline,
  and noticeable outer eyelashes.
- Thin light brown eyebrows, partially covered by the bangs. The nose is
  minimally indicated; the mouth is small and appears as a short, thin line
  in the neutral expression.
- Her default expression is calm and focused, with a slightly stern gaze.
  Expressions may change to suit the action while preserving her eye shape
  and facial features.
- Visible ears are small and normally rounded. Visible areas show no glasses,
  earrings, scars, tattoos, or makeup with bold patches of color.

### Hair and Its Fastening

- Warm champagne blonde hair matching the sit-down animation, with golden-beige
  midtones and tan-brown shadows. Highlights are light warm cream. Preserve
  the warm shaded strands rather than making the hair white or cool gray.
- A thick, high hairstyle with **one large ponytail** tied high at the back
  of her head. The ponytail first arches upward above its fastening, then
  falls behind her back in a broad, wavy mass reaching approximately to her
  hips; the apparent length of the tips varies with the viewing angle and
  the curvature of the strands.
- The ponytail consists of large distinct waves with S-shaped curves, pointed
  or curled tips, and a few thin loose strands. It does not resemble a smooth,
  straight ribbon or a braid.
- Voluminous, uneven bangs with separated locks extending over the forehead
  and partially framing the eyes. Shorter curved locks beside the face reach
  approximately to the cheeks and neck. Thin arching flyaway strands appear
  near the crown.
- The ponytail is fastened with a wide dark brown fabric hair tie with bulky
  folds, resembling a ruffle or gathered bow. A small gold diamond-shaped
  ornament with a red stone at its center is visible from the front/side.
- From the rear, the hair covers much of the upper back and cape. Preserve
  this volume and overlap when the character turns.

### Scarf and Cape

- A wide red scarf or folded collar wraps around her neck. It covers the base
  of the neck and lies over the upper breastplate. A narrow dark strip of an
  inner collar is visible near her chin.
- A large saturated red cape falls from the shoulders down her back, with
  deep burgundy shadows in the folds. From the front, its side panels frame
  her body; from the rear, they form a broad continuous mass.
- The cape is long, widens toward the bottom, and spreads sideways. Its tips
  reach approximately to the shins/ankles depending on the angle and how far
  the fabric fans out.
- The lower edge is distinctly worn: irregular pointed ends, large notches,
  and torn sections. Do not replace it with a straight, neatly hemmed edge.
- The fabric is matte, with broad readable folds and darker inner surfaces.
  The cape has no visible lettering, patterns, or large emblem.

### Torso and Shoulder Armor

- Dark brown fitted clothing beneath the armor, with soft folds and a texture
  resembling leather or heavy fabric on the arms and sides of the torso.
- A fitted silver breastplate with two rounded upper sections, a central seam,
  and a lower section tapering toward the waist. The side areas are dark;
  small gold details accent the joints and edges.
- A large gold emblem in the center of the chest: a decorative cross with
  widened, pointed arms and an elongated lower tip. Preserve its shape,
  color, and central placement rather than substituting an arbitrary symbol.
- Silver shoulder plates, rounded on top and composed of overlapping segments,
  with broad gold edging and round gold fasteners near the arms.
- The metal has pale silver surfaces, warm gray shadows, and broad bright
  highlights. Gold is warm, ochre in shadow, and pale yellow on lit edges.
  This is illustrated armor without photorealistic fine surface texture.

### Arms and Gloves

- The upper arms and elbow bends are predominantly dark brown; no exposed
  skin is visible between the shoulder armor and gloves.
- Silver forearm bracers, wider near the elbow and narrower toward the wrist,
  with gold rims, projections, and small fasteners.
- Dark brown gloves cover the palms and all fingers. Gold details near the
  wrists visually continue the bracers' trim.
- The hands are small, with stylized but distinct fingers. When changing the
  pose, preserve the gloves' volume and the armor construction on both arms.

### Belt, Pouch, and Lower Costume Layers

- Several overlapping brown belts around the waist, some at an angle, with
  rectangular gold buckles, rivets, and fasteners.
- A small brown leather pouch at the side of the belt, with a flap, rounded
  lower corners, a visible border/seam, and a gold clasp. In `front.png`,
  it is on the viewer's left, which is the heroine's right side.
- Short brown leather panels with gold details are visible beneath the belt,
  covering the top of the skirt near the hips.
- A short white or ivory skirt with folds, uneven layers, and a wavy hem.
- A red panel hangs at the center front, ending in a pointed tip and bearing
  a gold cross-shaped emblem. Additional pointed red fabric panels are
  visible at the sides over the white skirt.
- A small strip of fair thigh skin remains visible between the skirt hem
  and the tops of the stockings.

### Legs and Boots

- Dark brown stockings, almost black in shadow, extend above the knees.
  Their upper edges read as dark bands; do not replace them with continuous
  silver plate armor extending to the waist.
- Large silver knee guards with a shield/diamond shape, gold borders, and
  round gold fasteners at the sides.
- Elongated silver shin plates with a central ridge or bright facet, tapering
  near the ankles and bordered in gold.
- The boots have a dark brown base, silver plates over the toes and insteps,
  gold transverse straps/rims, and gold edging around the soles.
- Rounded toes extend slightly forward. The side views show low, broad heels;
  these are neither completely flat boots nor thin stiletto heels.
- Both legs have the same armor components. During movement, preserve the
  separation between the knee guard, shin protection, and armored foot.

### Palette and Illustration Technique

| Part | Main Color | Shadows and Accents |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hair | Warm champagne blonde | Golden-beige midtones, tan-brown shadows, light cream highlights |
| Skin | Fair peach | Pink cheeks, soft warm shadows |
| Eyes | Brown-amber | Dark upper areas, golden lower areas, white catchlights |
| Scarf, cape, fabric panels | Saturated red | Burgundy shadows, lighter red surfaces |
| Main armor | Silver | Warm gray shadows, almost white highlights |
| Trim and emblems | Warm gold | Ochre-brown shadows, yellow-cream highlights |
| Gloves, belts, pouch, underlayers | Dark brown | Lighter leather edges, deep brown shadows |
| Skirt | White / ivory | Pale gray and warm beige folds |

The canonical **hair color** comes from `../assets/characters/hero/sit_down.png`.
The front, rear and side references were recolored in PhotoCraft to match its
warm champagne highlights, golden-beige midtones and tan-brown shadows.
Use the current reference PNGs for new art; earlier generated animation sources may have older colors.

Color names are descriptive: take the exact shades from the images rather than
choosing arbitrary HEX codes. The style is a clean, illustrated 2D anime image
with chibi proportions, fine warm dark outlines, clear shadow shapes, and soft
transitions within large forms. Details are distinct, without a pixel grid,
3D rendering, or photorealism.

## Rules for New Images and Animations

1. **Review the references and attach them to the request before generation.**
   Text alone or a link to this README is not enough to communicate the
   character's appearance to the generation tool.
2. **Preserve the character's identity.** Her face shape, amber eyes, warm
   pale hair, single high ponytail and brown fastening, red scarf and tattered
   cape, silver-and-gold armor construction, emblems, belt, pouch, and skirt
   layers must remain recognizable in every pose.
3. **Describe the action separately from the appearance.** Change the pose,
   expression, gaze direction, and fabric movement to suit the task. Do not
   carry the T-pose into every result or redesign the character for a new action.
4. **For animation, add the relevant frames as supplementary motion references.**
   Materials in `walk_v2/`, `attack_v1/`, `sit_down_v1/`, `sharpen_v1/`,
   `death_v1/`, and `seated_v1/` can define poses, transitions, scale, and ground
   contact. The heroine's primary design is defined by `reference/`.
   When extending seated animations, also read the
   [rest animation contract](hero_rest.md).
5. **Maintain consistency between frames.** Do not change head or body size,
   hair length, armor shape, colors, belt count, or accessories. The hair and
   cape may sway, but must retain their attachment points, volume, and
   recognizable silhouettes. Emblems move with the surfaces they are on.
6. **Do not mirror asymmetric details without checking them.** When the
   character turns, account for her own left and right sides rather than only
   the sides of the image; check pouch placement and belt overlaps against
   the front reference.
7. **Specify the background according to the intended output.** The dark
   gradient and glow in the references are presentation elements. Game sprites
   require a transparent background without colored halos, background remnants,
   or cropped hair, cape, hands, boots, or weapons. For illustrations, describe
   the background separately.
8. **Provide a supplementary weapon reference when needed.** The heroine is
   unarmed in the T-poses; these three images do not define the weapon's shape.
   For a sword, use the relevant existing frame/action reference and preserve
   its design throughout the sequence.
9. **Visually review the result before use.** Compare the front, profile, and
   rear views; check the face, hairstyle, emblems, costume layers, and frame
   consistency. For a sprite sheet, also check frame order, cell dimensions,
   margins, and anchor point according to the specific animation contract.
   These references alone do not define FPS, dimensions, or anchors.

## Base Prompt

**Attach `front.png`, `back.png`, and `side.png`** to this text, then add the
specific action, viewing angle, and output format requirements:

> Depict the same heroine shown in the attached front, rear, and side references.
> Preserve her illustrated 2D anime/chibi style, large head and compact
> proportions, fair peach skin, large brown-amber eyes, warm creamy blonde
> hair matching the warm champagne palette of sit-down, with one voluminous
> high ponytail, wavy strands, and a dark brown fabric
> fastening with a gold ornament and red stone. Preserve the red scarf and
> long red cape with a tattered hem, silver armor with gold trim, gold
> cross-shaped chest emblem, dark brown gloves, belt with straps and a side
> pouch, short white pleated skirt with a red central panel and gold emblem,
> dark high stockings, and silver-and-gold armored boots. Reproduce the
> construction and details from the images. Change the pose to match the
> described action while preserving the character's appearance.

For animation, extend the prompt with the successive movement phases, frame
count, direction, scale and cell placement rules, and a transparent background
requirement. For a continuous loop, explicitly require the last frame to
connect smoothly to the first.
